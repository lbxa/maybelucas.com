I’m 16 and I’ve just written my first [neural net](https://mlp.lbxa.net).

It’s a multilayer perceptron. Inputs move forward through layers of weighted connections, the output is compared with the right answer and the error moves backwards. Then it does the same thing again. And again.

It can predict your performance in an exam based on how many hours you studied and slept before it.
